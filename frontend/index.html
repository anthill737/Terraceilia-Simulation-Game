Speak privately
Reaches one person alone. Everyone else hears nothing.
Move a person
They are found there with no memory of walking.
Set fire, or put one out
A fire burns for three days, ruins what is there, hurts the people, and spreads.
Destroy or restore a thing
A destroyed thing is gone and cannot be used. Restoring puts it back as if it had never been lost.
Strike someone down
They die at once, of no cause anyone can name. The dead stay dead unless you revive them under People.

Connect Telegram

Terraceilia messages you through a Telegram bot that belongs to you. Nobody else can read it and no account details are shared. Three short steps, about three minutes. The token is kept in telegram.json beside the game and is only ever sent to Telegram.
Step 1. Make the bot and paste its token
  1. Open Telegram on your phone or computer and search for @BotFather, the account with the blue check.
  2. Send it /newbot. It asks for a display name, which can be anything, for example Terraceilia. Then it asks for a username, which must end in bot, for example terraceilia_yourname_bot.
  3. BotFather replies with a token that looks like 123456789:AAH8f.... Copy the whole line and paste it below.
Step 2. Tell the bot who you are
  1. Open a chat with your new bot: its link appears here after step 1, or search its username in Telegram.
  2. Press Start, then send it any message, for example hi. A bot is not allowed to message you until you message it first.
  3. Come back here and press Find my chat.
